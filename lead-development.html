Lead Concierge Development Track

The path from Concierge to Senior Agent to Lead Concierge — and on to Trainer Certified. What each stage means, how it is measured, how to grow, and the gate to the next.

Draft v1 · Pending approval by the Regional Concierge Manager Module 5 · Ongoing Development
The Path
Four stages. One standard.
Every stage is measured against the same Apex Professional Standards Framework (APSF). The standard does not change as you move up. What changes is how many people you hold to it.
How leaders are expected to lead
Lead by example
Model the behavior and professionalism you expect from your team.
Foster collaboration
Create an environment where team members share ideas and support one another.
Commit to growth
Embrace feedback, refine processes, and inspire your team to keep improving.
The four APSF levels — used at every stage
Level 1
Incomplete
The standard was not met. The work fails its basic purpose.
Level 2
Developing
Partially met. Would not hold up under pressure, handoff or review.
Level 3
Apex Standard
The expectation and the certification threshold — not extra credit.
Level 4
Witness Quality
Could stand in a review unedited. Sustained Level 4 identifies future leaders.
Scoring rules that carry into leadership
  • No averaging. Every dimension is scored on its own. A 4 in one cannot cover a 1 in another.
  • The lowest dimension is the coaching priority. Coaching always starts there.
  • Score the work, not the person. A profile is the pattern across at least three scored samples.
  • Evidence rule. Every score below Level 3 cites the specific entry, or absence, behind it.
This track is Draft v1. The stage definitions draw on approved Apex sources; the gates between stages and the Lead self-assessment anchors are new and pending approval by the Regional Concierge Manager.

Grounded in: Apex Professional Standards Framework v1.0 (levels, scoring rules, usage map, Competency 05 draft dimensions) · Senior Agent Peer Coaching Guide · The Heart of the Apex Standard ("Your Role as a Leader") · NHE Speed packet (path from day one, 90-day milestone).

Stage 01
Concierge
The foundation every leader is built on. Nobody skips it.

What it means

Cleared through NHE Speed — 80% or higher on the final assessment, then a Lead sign-off after showing readiness on shift. The concierge runs the post to the Apex Standard: the same H.O.M.E. values at 8 AM, at midnight, and on a quiet overnight. The first 90 days are the performance window.

Days 1–3Shadow
Days 4–7Supported
Week 2Solo + check-ins
Weeks 3–4Full operation
Day 30Formal review
Day 90Full evaluation

What you're measured on

  • Attendance, log quality, resident feedback and presence
  • Documentation Excellence (APSF Competency 01): Level 3 on all five dimensions plus a passing Witness Test at the 30-day field confirmation
  • Quarterly audit: 2–3 sampled logs per quarter keep certification current
  • Annual Escalation, Emergency & De-Escalation recertification

Readiness signals

  • Clean attendance, strong logs, no conduct flags
  • Holds the standard when no Lead is present
  • Takes action on visible problems without being prompted
  • Escalates without hesitation — and documents it
  • Level 4 (Witness Quality) entries start to appear in audits

Development activities

  • Shift Log Mastery — move from Level 3 to Level 4
  • SOP Refreshers — packages, incidents, the desk
  • De-Escalation Recertification — annual
  • Excellence Academy — when eligible and nominated

Gate to Senior Agent Proposed

  • Day-90 full evaluation complete: clean attendance, strong logs, no conduct flags, supervisor endorsement — the Excellence Academy eligibility standard
  • Excellence Academy completed
  • Endorsed by the Regional Concierge Manager

Sources: NHE Speed packet (sign-off, path from today, Day-90 Excellence Academy eligibility) · APSF 2.9 certification and 3.2 quarterly audit · Peer Coaching Guide Module 09 "Ready Looks Like".

Stage 02
Senior Agent
The real-world standard, lived and modeled in front of the agent you are developing.

What it means

The Senior Agent is a peer coach. Training does not end at sign-off — the Senior Agent carries it onto the floor. They coach new hires through the nine modules of the Peer Coaching Guide across the first 3–5 shifts, run performance refreshers when an agent needs a reset, and complete the Agent Readiness Sign-Off that goes to the Regional Manager before a new hire's first solo shift. They demonstrate each standard before asking the agent to replicate it.

What you're measured on

  • Your own APSF profile — still Level 3 or higher on every dimension, no averaging
  • The agents you clear: do they hold the standard at their 30-day field confirmation?
  • Sign-offs completed module by module against "Ready Looks Like" — not on impression
  • Coaching that names the dimension and level, never general feedback

Readiness signals

  • A sustained Level 4 (Witness Quality) profile — APSF names this as the marker for Documentation Mentor candidates
  • Coaches by dimension: "this entry is a 2 on Outcome Quality — here's what a 3 looks like"
  • Names A.C.T. out loud in debriefs, and where it broke down
  • Agents they sign off still meet the standard at 30 days

Development activities

  • Peer Coaching Guide — all nine modules, three use modes
  • Score anonymized logs on the APSF rubric, then compare with your Lead
  • Shadow a Lead's site walk and coach block
  • De-Escalation Recertification — model it, don't just pass it

Gate to Lead Concierge Proposed

  • Sustained Level 4 profile across at least three scored samples
  • At least one complete Agent Readiness Sign-Off whose new hire passed the 30-day field confirmation
  • Lead self-assessment at Level 3 or higher on Shift Ownership and Judgment Modeling
  • Endorsed by the Regional Concierge Manager

Sources: Senior Agent Peer Coaching Guide (note before you begin, how to use, sign-off sheet) · APSF 1.5 scoring rules and 3.1 usage map (live coaching, leadership development).

Stage 03
Lead Concierge
You are the champion of the Apex Standard at your site. Your guidance sets the tone for the team.

What it means

The Lead owns the site's standard. In the reporting chain — Agent → Site Lead → Regional Manager → Executive Leadership — the Lead receives escalations, reviews incident reports for accuracy and detail, and follows up when something needs clarifying. The Lead runs the NHE Speed site shadow, initials the walk checklist, and clears new hires for solo only after they show readiness on shift. The Lead leads by example, fosters collaboration, and commits to growth.

What you're measured on Draft

  • APSF Competency 05 — Operational Leadership (draft dimensions):
  • Coaching Against Standards
  • Audit Calibration
  • Shift Ownership
  • Facilitation Readiness
  • Judgment Modeling
  • Site outcomes: audit scores, new-hire 30-day results, open loops in pass-on

Readiness signals for trainer

  • Level 3 or higher on all five Operational Leadership dimensions
  • Level 4 on Facilitation Readiness and Audit Calibration
  • Scores logs within one level of the Regional Concierge Manager
  • The site holds the standard on the Lead's days off

Development activities

  • Lead Self-Assessment — find your lowest dimension
  • 90-Day Lead Plan — build it around that dimension
  • Peer Coaching Guide — coach your Senior Agents to coach
  • NHE Speed Facilitator Flow — learn the live segments

Gate to Trainer Certified

  • Meets the criteria on the Trainer Certification page
  • A completed 90-day Lead plan with all three check-ins signed

Sources: The Heart of the Apex Standard ("Your Role as a Leader") · Incident Reporting SOP (reporting flow) · NHE Speed packet (site shadow, sign-off) · APSF 4.2 competency roadmap (05 Operational Leadership, draft) and 3.3 calibration requirement.

Stage 04
Trainer Certified
If it cannot be run cold by someone who has never met the author, it is not finished.

What it means

A Trainer Certified leader delivers the live segments of NHE Speed — kickoff, coach block and sign-off — and scores work against APSF with calibration to the Regional Concierge Manager. APSF calls these leaders "Trainer Qualified": people who can inherit and operate the framework without rebuilding it. Training can start at any hour; a certified trainer can run it on any shift.

What you're measured on

  • New-hire outcomes: final assessment results and 30-day field confirmations
  • Scoring calibration against the Regional Concierge Manager
  • Fidelity to the program — delivered as written, pending items flagged as pending

Development activities

  • Trainer Certification — the full criteria
  • Co-Facilitator App — co-run a session first
  • NHE Speed Facilitator Flow

Keeping it

  • Certification is maintained by continued delivery and quarterly calibration. If calibration drifts, re-calibrate before the next session.

Sources: APSF 1.2 culture loop and 4.2 design-for-inheritance principle · NHE Speed program structure.
