A note before you begin

Training does not end at sign-off.

This guide exists because training does not end at sign-off. As a Senior Agent or Lead Concierge, you carry something the slide deck cannot deliver: the real-world standard, lived and modeled in front of the agent you are developing.

Use this guide as a structure, not a script. Each section tells you what to cover, how to demonstrate it, and what ready looks like before you sign off. The agent you are developing should leave every interaction knowing exactly where they stand and what they are working toward.

This guide works for three situations: new hire shadow shifts in the first week after NHE Speed sign-off, performance refreshers when an agent needs a reset, and self-directed review when no Lead is present. The standard is the same in all three.
