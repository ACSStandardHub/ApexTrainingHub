Emergency drill · first three steps

Fire: don't investigate, call 911 then supervisor · pull the alarm · exits, no elevators.

Medical: call 911 with address, floor, unit · don't move them · stay with them.

Security: don't confront, keep distance · 911 if credible · notify supervisor. Safety first.

Systems match

Connecteam: clock in/out, shift swap, incident report.

Paylocity: pay stub, direct deposit, W-2.

Property system: work order, verify resident before release.

Final assessment

  • 14 multiple-choice (1 pt each) + written escalation entry (6 pts) = 20
  • 16 or higher certifies. The packet shows a certificate.
  • Below 16: review the missed items, then re-take before signing item 9
  • Lowest category on the result screen = 30-day focus area

Written entry is auto-checked for

  • A valid time stamp
  • A full Event sentence
  • Both unit numbers in the Detail
  • Who was notified, with time or channel
  • A closed loop (report filed, pass-on, pending)
  • Third-person, no opinions

Read it yourself too. The checks catch format, not judgment.

Solo clearance

The assessment shows knowledge. The Lead clears for solo only after the hire demonstrates readiness on shift: Days 1–3 shadow, Days 4–7 supported, Week 2 solo with daily check-ins.

Only 8 hours

  • Module 1 to 60 min (skip the reflection)
  • Module 4 to 50 min
  • Coach block to 75 min: four scenarios (drop 23 or 22)
  • Assessment to 40 min

Up to 10 hours

  • Shadow to 120 min: hire runs the desk 30 min, Lead watching
  • Coach block adds the bonus scenarios (24, 25)

Evening or overnight start

  • Same order, same lengths
  • Line up a Lead or Senior Agent who is on site for the shadow block
  • Walk the property as it runs at that hour: lighting, locked doors, overnight package process

Two or more hires

  • One device each; progress saves per device
  • Run kickoff, check-ins, coach block and sign-off together
  • Split them across Leads for the shadow block if needed
