What the Academy is

Leadership, practiced on shift.

The Apex Excellence Academy develops the agents who will lead Apex sites next. It is executive in tone and site-ready in practice: every week has one theme, one field challenge carried out on real shifts, and one honest reflection. There is no classroom and no homework. The work is the shift.

The Academy is

  • Twelve weeks, one theme per week
  • Applied on live shifts at your own site
  • Measured across six leadership dimensions
  • Facilitated by the Regional Concierge Manager, with a 1:1 pulse check
  • A step toward Senior Agent, Lead and trainer roles

The Academy is not

  • An academic course or a lecture series
  • Homework outside your shift
  • A reward for tenure alone
  • A replacement for the Apex Standard — it builds on it

"The future site leaders at Apex are sitting somewhere in our company right now — performing well, operating with integrity, and not waiting to be asked."

The Leadership Seed · Apex New Hire Experience
Who it's for

Agents who have proven the foundation.

The Academy is for concierge agents who have completed the New Hire Experience, held the standard through their first 90 days, and are ready to grow beyond onboarding. Eligibility is assessed at the Day-90 full evaluation, the end of probation.

01
Clean attendance
No pattern of tardiness, unexcused absences or no-call / no-shows across the first 90 days.
02
Strong logs
Shift logs at the Apex Standard — E.D.O. on every entry, complete pass-ons, escalations documented.
03
No conduct flags
Clean against the conduct, device, confidentiality and fraternization standards.
04
Supervisor endorsement
A Site Lead or supervisor puts their name behind the agent.

Source: NHE Speed packet, "Your path from today" — Day 90: trajectory and Excellence Academy eligibility. The Academy app does not set separate criteria.

How nomination works

Nominated, not self-enrolled.

  1. Endorsement at Day 90 or later

    The Site Lead or supervisor confirms the four eligibility criteria at the agent's Day-90 full evaluation, or at any later review.

  2. Regional review

    The Regional Concierge Manager reviews the evidence: the agent's documentation profile, attendance and conduct record, and the endorsement.

  3. Cohort placement

    Confirmed agents are placed in the next cohort and given access to the Academy app.

  4. Baseline

    The agent completes the Leadership Self-Assessment — 18 questions across the six dimensions — so growth is measured from a real starting point.

What the Academy measures

Six leadership dimensions.

Every week, every scenario and every reflection builds one of six dimensions. The app shows each participant their full profile across all six.

1

Presence & Professionalism

How you show up before you say a word. Uniform, energy, the shift-start ritual.

2

Communication & Tone

Regulate first, then respond. Word choice, volume, and what your silence communicates.

3

Operational Discipline

The invisible work — logs, handoffs, follow-through. If it isn't documented, it didn't happen.

4

Ownership & Problem-Solving

From explaining to owning. Root cause before reaction. Your name on the outcome.

5

Delegation & Coaching

Stop fixing. Start developing. The question instead of the answer.

6

Judgment & Decision-Making

Prioritization, escalation thresholds, and owning the call.

Program structure

Twelve weeks. One theme at a time.

Each week follows the same rhythm inside the Academy app.

Week Feed

The week's theme, its focus, and one field challenge to carry out on shift.

Scenario Trainer

Real situations, honest choices, sharp feedback — practiced on your phone between tasks.

Weekly Reflection

An honest end-of-week account, shared with the facilitator.

My Commitment

One active focus at a time, with a history of past commitments.

My Progress

Your profile across all six leadership dimensions.

Facilitator view

The facilitator tracks cohort activity and the gap that shapes the next week's content.

Phase labels below are for orientation on this page. The app itself runs week by week.

Weeks 1–3Foundation
Week 1
Presence & Professionalism
How you show up before you say a word. Uniform standard. Energy management. The shift-start ritual.
Field challengeFor five consecutive shifts, be fully set — desk clean, log reviewed, building status known — before the first thing asks something of you.
Week 2
Communication & Tone
Regulate first, then respond. Word choice, volume, and what your silence communicates. Translating competence into presence.
Field challengeIdentify one interaction per shift where you redirect a conversation — log what you said and how it landed.
Week 3
Operational Discipline
The invisible work. Shift logs, handoffs, follow-through. If it isn't documented, it didn't happen.
Field challengeEnd every shift this week with a complete log — detailed enough that anyone could pick up exactly where you left off.
Week 4Pulse check
Week 4
Pulse Check — 1:1s
Individual coaching conversations with the facilitator. No group content this week.
Field challengeBe honest in your 1:1. Come with one thing you're proud of and one thing you're working through.
Weeks 5–9Development
Week 5
Vocal Presence
Communicating with your team, not just to residents. Briefing up. Speaking first. Making your thinking audible.
Field challengeEach shift this week, say one thing out loud to your team that you would normally have kept to yourself.
Week 6
Ownership & Problem-Solving
The shift from explaining to owning. Root cause before reaction. Your name on the outcome.
Field challengeWhen something goes wrong, write one sentence: what you'll do differently. No context. No explanation. Just the move.
Week 7
Delegation & Coaching
Stop fixing. Start developing. The question instead of the answer. Trusting someone else's outcome.
Field challengeDelegate one task this week that you'd normally absorb. Let the outcome be theirs.
Week 8
Reading the Room
Environmental scanning. Anticipating before being asked. The concierge who knows before the resident does.
Field challengeAt shift start each day, write three things you observe that haven't been said yet.
Week 9
Judgment & Decision-Making
Escalation thresholds. Owning the call. The prioritization sequence:
Safety→Resident Experience→Team→Admin
Field challengeAfter every significant decision, write one sentence explaining your reasoning. Build the habit of knowing why.
Week 10Integration
Week 10
Integration — Putting It Together
All six dimensions active at once. This is what leadership looks like at full capacity.
Field challengeLead one full shift this week as if it were your evaluation. Log everything. Own everything.
Weeks 11–12Evaluation & completion
Week 11
Evaluation Week I
Formal evaluation. Weeks 1–10 in action. The facilitator is watching with intention.
Field challengeShow up as the version of yourself you've been building toward. No performance — just application.
Week 12
Evaluation Week II + Completion
Final evaluation. Program completion. Recognition of growth across all dimensions.
Field challengeFinish strong. The last shift matters as much as the first.
