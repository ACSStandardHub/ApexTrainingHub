Trainer Certification Framework

How a Lead or Senior Agent is certified to facilitate NHE Speed and to coach new hires with the Peer Coaching Guide: who is eligible, the path to certification, the rubric, and how certification is kept.

This is a new framework. Nothing on this page is policy until it is approved. It is built on existing Apex systems: the Apex Professional Standards Framework (levels, scoring rules and the draft Competency 05 dimensions), the NHE Speed Facilitator Flow, the Senior Agent Peer Coaching Guide, and the 90-day eligibility standard in the NHE Speed packet.

Purpose

One standard for the people who teach the standard.

Every new hire meets Apex through a trainer. If trainers teach to different standards, new hires leave with different standards. Certification makes sure every Lead or Senior Agent who runs NHE Speed or signs an Agent Readiness Sign-Off teaches, coaches and scores the same way the Regional Manager would.

Trainers are scored on APSF Competency 05, Operational Leadership, using the same four levels every agent already knows: Incomplete, Developing, Apex Standard, Witness Quality.

What certification allows

Lead an NHE Speed run as facilitator. Coach new hires through their shadow shifts with the Peer Coaching Guide and sign the Agent Readiness Sign-Off.

Who certifies

The Regional Concierge Manager observes, scores, calibrates and signs. Certification is recorded on the observation scorecard at the end of this page.

The threshold

Level 3 Apex Standard or higher on all five Operational Leadership dimensions, plus a PASS on the Verdict Test. The same rule APSF uses for agents.

Certification pathway

Five stages, in order.

Each stage is cleared before the next begins. NHE Speed can start at any hour, so any stage can run on an evening or overnight start.

  1. 1

    Observe

    Watch one full NHE Speed run, kickoff to sign-off, following along in the Facilitator Flow block by block.

    Evidence
    Notes on each block’s “Before you move on” checks, plus one question per block.
    Clears when
    The candidate debriefs the run with the Regional Manager.
  2. 2

    Co-facilitate

    Co-facilitate a run with the Regional Manager using the Facilitator Flow. The candidate owns the coach block’s entry review, at least two required scenarios, and the in-person handoff to the shadow Lead.

    Evidence
    Regional Manager notes on the blocks the candidate led.
    Clears when
    The Regional Manager confirms the candidate is ready to lead.

    The legacy 3-day Co-Facilitator App stays available as a reference for talk tracks.

  3. 3

    Lead under observation

    Lead a full run. The Regional Manager observes and scores it on the scorecard below. Then coach that hire’s shadow shifts with the Peer Coaching Guide and submit the Agent Readiness Sign-Off.

    Evidence
    Completed scorecard and the hire’s Agent Readiness Sign-Off.
    Clears when
    Level 3+ on all five dimensions and a Verdict Test PASS.
  4. 4

    Calibrate

    Score three anonymized shift logs on your own against APSF Competency 01. The Regional Manager scores the same three.

    Evidence
    Both sets of scores, with evidence for every score below Level 3.
    Clears when
    Within one level of the Regional Manager on every dimension of every log, with the same Witness Test verdict.
  5. 5

    Certified

    The Regional Manager signs the scorecard. The trainer may lead NHE Speed runs and sign Agent Readiness Sign-Offs. Maintenance begins.

    Evidence
    Signed scorecard on file.
    Kept by
    Annual re-observation and quarterly re-calibration.
Evaluation rubric · APSF Competency 05 · Operational Leadership

Five dimensions, four levels.

The dimensions are the draft Competency 05 set from the APSF roadmap. Each is scored on the same four universal levels used everywhere at Apex.

Level 1 · IncompleteThe standard was not met.
Level 2 · DevelopingPartly met. Would not hold up alone.
Level 3 · Apex StandardThe expectation and the certification threshold.
Level 4 · Witness QualityCould be used to train other trainers.
Scoring rules

Scored the way APSF scores a log.

  • No averaging. Each dimension is scored on its own and reported as five scores, not one number. A 4 in Facilitation Readiness cannot make up for a 1 in Judgment Modeling.
  • Certification threshold: Level 3 or higher on all five dimensions, plus a PASS on the Verdict Test.
  • The lowest dimension is the development focus. Feedback after every observation starts there.
  • Evidence rule. Every score below Level 3 cites the specific moment, or the absence, that justifies it. If the observer cannot point to it, the score does not stand.
  • Score the observation, not the person. Levels describe what was seen on one run. Certification rests on the Stage 3 observation plus the Stage 4 calibration.

The Verdict Test

Asked after the five dimensions are scored
“Would I put a new hire in front of this trainer alone tomorrow?”

PASS or FAIL, with one sentence of justification. This is a separate judgment, not a summary of the scores. A trainer can reach Level 3 on paper and still fail it. A run fails the Verdict Test when, despite adequate scores, the trainer:

  • taught something that contradicts an SOP and did not correct it before the hire left;
  • cleared a checklist or sign-off item they did not actually observe;
  • crossed Friendly ≠ Familiar or broke confidentiality in front of the hire;
  • left the post, or the hire, without coverage.
Keeping certification

Maintenance, suspension and the road back

RequirementStandard
Annual re-observationOne full NHE Speed run observed and scored by the Regional Manager within 12 months of certification, then every year. Same scorecard, same threshold.
Quarterly re-calibrationEach quarter, alongside the quarterly audit, score three anonymized logs. Stay within one level of the Regional Manager on every dimension.
Own documentationKept through quarterly audit sampling, as APSF does for every agent. The trainer’s own logs stay at Level 3 or higher.
StandingCertified trainers stay eligible for the Excellence Academy standard: clean attendance, strong logs, no conduct flags.

What triggers suspension

  • Own logs drop below Level 3 on any APSF Documentation dimension in a quarterly audit, or fail the Witness Test.
  • Calibration more than one level off the Regional Manager in two consecutive quarters.
  • Trainee audit pattern: two or more agents this trainer signed off score Level 1–2 on the same dimension at their 30-day review.
  • A Verdict Test FAIL on any observed run, an active conduct flag, or a no-call no-show.

While suspended, the trainer does not lead NHE Speed runs or sign Agent Readiness Sign-Offs. They may co-facilitate with the Regional Manager.

The road back

Targeted work on the dimension that triggered the suspension, with the Regional Manager. Own-log issues follow the APSF remediation pathway.
Re-check against the same anchors: re-audit, re-calibration, or a co-facilitated run followed by an observed lead run.
The Regional Manager reinstates on a scorecard marked “Reinstatement.” Remediation is the road back to certified, not a separate credential.
