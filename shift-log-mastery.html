Reference Card

Subject line

[Site] — [Date] — [Shift Time] — [Your Name]Example: Harbor Point — 10/08/26 — 3 PM–11 PM — Agent Name. Sign off at the bottom with your name and shift time. Submit to the PASS ON LOG email group at end of shift; add recipients by hand if it doesn't auto-fill.

E.D.O. — every entry, every time

EEvent. What happened? One specific, observable sentence. If you can't state it clearly, you don't have an Event yet.
DDetail. Who, where, when, how? Unit or location, exact time, parties involved. Facts only.
OOutcome. What did you do? Resolved, escalated or pending. If pending: to whom, and why.

Every Outcome closes in four parts

  1. The action taken
  2. Who was notified — by name and channel
  3. The status — resolved, escalated or pending
  4. The next step, and its owner

Three questions before every entry

  1. What happened?
  2. Do I have the who, where and when?
  3. What did I do about it?

Non-negotiables

  • At least 1 time-stamped entry per hour. If nothing occurred, log what you checked.
  • At least 2 site tours: route, stops, observations.
  • Pass-on: all three sections every shift — Concierge, Maintenance, Leasing.
  • Third person, neutral, factual. "Concierge greeted resident…", never "I told them…".
  • Corrections are additions — a follow-up entry with the time. Never delete. Never copy-paste.

The four levels

  • Level 1 Incomplete — fails its basic purpose.
  • Level 2 Developing — exists, but won't hold up under handoff or review.
  • Level 3 Apex Standard — the expectation.
  • Level 4 Witness Quality — could stand in a review unedited.

The Witness Test — four fatal flaws

  • An open loop with no owner
  • A liability-creating statement — opinion, blame, speculation
  • A silent escalation — leadership involved, no record of who, when or why
  • A timeline that cannot be reconstructed
